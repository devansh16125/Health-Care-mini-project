SLIDE 3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[SPEAKER NOTES]:
Here we define our problem statement and 4 core objectives. Notice problem number one: data leakage. Wen et al. in Medical Image Analysis 2020 showed that almost half of published studies suffer from data leakage because scans from the same patient appear in both train and test sets. Our objective is to build a mathematically sound, leak-free pipeline, benchmark 6 models, prove the value of MRI through ablation, and provide game-theoretic SHAP explanations.

  [Text Shape 0 (TextBox 1)]:
RESEARCH SCOPE & OBJECTIVES

  [Text Shape 1 (TextBox 2)]:
Problem Statement & Core Research Objectives

  [Text Shape 3 (TextBox 4)]:
The Central Research Challenges
1. Data Leakage Crisis: Numerous published neuroimaging ML papers report inflated accuracies (>95%) because repeat scans of the same participant are mixed across train/test splits (Wen et al., 2020).
2. Computational Barrier: Raw 3D volumetric CNNs require costly GPU clusters and extensive training hours, limiting bedside translation.
3. The Black-Box Dilemma: Complex ensembles make unexplainable predictions, failing clinical verification and regulatory trust.

  [Text Shape 5 (TextBox 6)]:
Target Research Objectives
Obj 1 - Zero-Leakage Pipeline: Design and enforce strict subject-level holdout partitioning (N=188 train, N=47 test) and scikit-learn pipeline encapsulation.
Obj 2 - Multimodal Fusion Benchmark: Evaluate 6 classical and ensemble learning models (Dummy, LR, RF, SVM, GB, Soft Voting) combining clinical and structural MRI features.
Obj 3 - Modality Ablation Analysis: Quantify the exact incremental diagnostic gain of structural MRI volumetrics over clinical psychometrics alone.
Obj 4 - Game-Theoretic Explainability: Deploy TreeSHAP to generate global biomarker importance distributions and transparent patient-level local waterfall explanations.

  [Text Shape 0 (TextBox 1)]:
PREPROCESSING & FEATURE DERIVATION

  [Text Shape 1 (TextBox 2)]:
Feature Preprocessing & Neuroimaging Engineering

  [Text Shape 3 (TextBox 4)]:
Engineered Neuroimaging Indices
1. Brain Atrophy Index: Defined as (1.0 - nWBV). Direct inverse of normalized brain volume; directly measures parenchymal tissue loss percentage.
2. Normalized Intracranial Volume: Calculated as (eTIV * ASF) / 1000. Provides calibrated cranial cavity normalization across head sizes.
3. Cognitive Brain Reserve: Interaction product (MMSE * nWBV). Models the clinical buffer hypothesis—higher brain volume mitigates cognitive deficits.

  [Text Shape 5 (TextBox 6)]:
Zero-Leakage Transformation Pipeline
SimpleImputer(strategy='median'): Fits median SES exclusively on training fold. Test missing values receive the training median, preventing test distribution leakage.
StandardScaler(): Calculates μ_train and σ_train on training fold. Test features scaled via x_test_norm = (x_test - μ_train) / σ_train.
OneHotEncoder(drop='first'): Encodes biological sex into binary Gender_Male (0=Female, 1=Male).
Ablation Subsets: Features grouped into Clinical-Only (5 feats), MRI-Only (4 feats), and Multimodal (9 feats).

SLIDE 9
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[SPEAKER NOTES]:
Slide 9 establishes the theory behind SHAP. Why SHAP instead of simple Gini importance? Gini importance is biased toward high-cardinality features and lacks additivity. SHAP satisfies the four fundamental axioms of cooperative game theory: Efficiency, Symmetry, Dummy, and Additivity. TreeSHAP computes exact Shapley values in polynomial time, enabling both global biomarker audits and individualized waterfall plots.

  [Text Shape 0 (TextBox 1)]:
THEORETICAL EXPLAINABILITY FRAMEWORK

  [Text Shape 1 (TextBox 2)]:
Explainable AI (XAI) Using Game-Theoretic SHAP

  [Text Shape 3 (TextBox 4)]:
Mathematical Foundations of SHAP
Unified Attribution: SHAP connects game-theoretic Shapley values with local surrogate explanations (Lundberg & Lee, 2017).
Additive Decomposition: Model output is decomposed as: f(x) = φ₀ + Σ φᵢ(x), where φ₀ is baseline expectation and φᵢ is feature contribution.
Axiom 1 - Efficiency: Sum of feature attributions equals the difference between model output and expected baseline.
Axiom 2 - Symmetry: Features contributing identically across all subsets receive identical attribution.
Axiom 3 - Additivity: For ensemble models, feature attributions sum linearly across component trees: φᵢ(f + g) = φᵢ(f) + φᵢ(g).

  [Text Shape 5 (TextBox 6)]:
TreeSHAP & Clinical Translation
Polynomial Complexity: TreeSHAP calculates exact attributions in O(T L D²) time instead of exponential time, making it practical for real-time clinical screening.
Global Feature Audit: Calculates mean absolute SHAP across all test patients, revealing which biological markers universally drive dementia predictions.
Patient-Level Explanations: Waterfall plots show exact risk factors pushing an individual patient toward or away from a dementia diagnosis.
Eliminating 'Black-Box' Risk: Enables clinicians to verify that predictions align with known neuropathology rather than spurious dataset artifacts.
